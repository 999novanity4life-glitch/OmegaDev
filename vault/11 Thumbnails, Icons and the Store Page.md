# 11 Thumbnails, Icons and the Store Page

Fact: a custom thumbnail is required to get homepage recommendations; play-through rate (clicks after impressions) is a top discovery signal ([discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery.md)).

Studio rules:
- Icon: one character or object, big face/emotion, 2–3 strong colors, readable at 50 px. Max 3 words of text.
- Thumbnails: show the core verb and the best reward; the "huge number" and "rare reveal" moments convert.
- Make 3+ variants and A/B them (Roblox supports thumbnail personalization/testing in the Creator Dashboard — check current docs before relying on it).
- Title: genre word + hook ("Anime" or other IP names you don't own are not allowed).
- Description: first line = what you do; then updates; then codes/social.
