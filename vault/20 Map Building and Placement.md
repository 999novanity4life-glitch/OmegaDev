# 20 Map Building and Placement

How to build a map that looks designed, not dumped. Full rules with IDs: `docs/rulebooks/MAP_RULEBOOK.md`. Repo survey: `docs/research/MAP_BUILDING_REPOS_2026-11-19.md`. Video lessons: `docs/research/videos-map-building/`. Layout and wayfinding theory: chapter 16. Lighting: chapter 15.

## The ten rules that matter most
1. **Reference board first**, shown to Sharman and approved before the blockout.
2. **Three scales in order:** macro (50-300+ studs, reads from far) → meso (10-40, frames routes) → micro (under 8, near the camera and at edges). Never fix emptiness with scatter.
3. **Every object has a reason** (frame, guide, block, tell, rest). No reason, delete it.
4. **A weenie ends every path:** tall, bright, moving or unique in colour. Motion pulls hardest.
5. **Poisson-disc scatter, then deliberate clumps**; vary yaw, scale, tilt and ground contact; keep-clear zones on spawns and lanes.
6. **"What happened here"** sentence and 3-5 props that prove it, per area.
7. **Mesh first.** Parts only for greybox placeholders, each one a tracked debt.
8. **Nothing at y = 0**; terrain on multiples of 4; no rectangular water.
9. **Glow for focus only**; Atmosphere needs a Sky; Density 0.08-0.15 outdoors; one lighting change at a time.
10. **Fixed screenshot stations** (spawn, play, far, top-down, orbit) + blind visual review + a walked playtest before anything is called done.
