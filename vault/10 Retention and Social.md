# 10 Retention and Social

Facts: discovery uses play days per user (D1, D2–7, D8–28), playtime (capped at 60 min/day), co-play with friends, spend days ([discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery.md)). Single-player games often struggle to keep an audience ([design for Roblox](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/game-design/design-for-roblox)).

Studio rules:
- Daily reward calendar (streak, escalating), daily quests (3, quick), playtime gifts.
- Group/like/favorite rewards must not gate core content.
- Friends: party boost when playing together, quick-join for raids/co-op, trading if the economy supports it.
- Server-wide moments: announce rare rolls in chat or a small banner, not a centre pop-up for everyone.
- Every teaser and notification must be true; check them after every update.
