# Video study, 2026-10-28

Sharman asked for the videos that teach how to make **high-quality** games, not low-effort Roblox clones. 27 videos were picked; **16 have notes** (captions read in full and summarised with timestamps), **11 are not covered yet** because YouTube rate-limited the caption downloads. Nothing below is claimed for a video without notes.

How the notes were made: captions were downloaded on Pranay's Mac (YouTube is blocked from the cloud container) and summarised point by point with timestamps. "Manual" captions were written by the uploader; "auto" are YouTube's speech recognition and can garble names. Every "Applies to Roblox how" line is our inference, not the speaker's claim.

Where the lessons went: lighting and look in `vault/15 Lighting and Realism.md`, maps in `vault/16 Level Design and Wayfinding.md`, UI and motion in `vault/05 UI and HUD.md`, feel in `vault/07 Characters, VFX and Assets.md`, review in `docs/VISUAL_QA.md`.
