# Pitch 4: Grow a Potion Garden

**One sentence a kid gets:** Plant magic seeds, brew potions, sell them.

Data captured 2026-10-23. "Inferred" = my judgment.

## Why now (Steam signal)
- **Witchbrook** (Chucklefish): magic-school life sim; brew potions, sell them at your shop and the Sunday market, solo or 4-player co-op. 8th most-wishlisted on Steam per SteamDB, via https://www.notebookcheck.net/Hogwarts-Legacy-meets-Stardew-Valley-New-magic-school-game-climbs-wishlist-charts-on-Steam.1348980.0.html . 117.2K followers, release about 2 months out per https://profitable.app/steam/upcoming .

## Familiar Roblox loop
- **Grow a Garden**: buy seeds, plant, harvest, sell, buy rarer seeds. Peak 22.3M concurrent on 2025-08-23. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games . Now 16.8K (https://levelupplay.my/charts/top-playing), so the loop is well known but the original has cooled (inferred: room for a fresh take).

## Loop
Plant a seed. Wait. Harvest a glowing herb. Drop herbs in the cauldron to brew a potion. Sell it at your market stall. Buy rarer seeds.

## Where the depth comes from
- Cauldron brewing animation; wrong mixes explode in colored smoke (harmless, funny).
- Potions do visible things: grow your garden, shrink a friend, make you float.
- Weekly "Magic Exam" event with a short cutscene and a limited seed.

## Monetization
Seed packs with every result and odd shown and PolicyService respected (rule 7), extra plots, robes and wands, faster grow pass.

## v1 scope
10 seeds, 5 recipes, cauldron, stall with NPC buyers, 1 weekly event.

## Risks
Don't use Witchbrook or Hogwarts names. Garden games are crowded; the brewing step is what has to feel new (inferred).
