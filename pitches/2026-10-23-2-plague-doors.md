**One sentence a kid gets:** Open the next door, and don't get caught.

Data captured 2026-10-23. "Inferred" = my judgment.

## Familiar Roblox loop
- **DOORS**: survival horror, go room by room and hide from monsters. All-time peak 274K in **October 2026**, 7.29B visits, 92.9% likes. https://rowatcher.com/games/2440500124/doors . Up 15.2% in 24h on https://statroblox.com/leaderboards/playing (49.9K at snapshot).

## Loop
Enter a room, find the key, open the next door. When the bells ring, hide (barrel, wardrobe, haystack) before the Plague Knight charges through. Reach door 100.

## Where the depth comes from
- Each monster gets an entrance animation and a sound cue kids learn.
- Cutscenes at door 50 (the bridge collapses) and door 100 (the plague heart boss).
- Lantern flicker, breath, camera shake (respect ReducedMotion).

## Monetization
Revives (developer product, as DOORS does), lantern and cloak cosmetics, private lobbies. No paid random items in v1.

## v1 scope
50 procedurally chained rooms from 12 hand-built pieces, 3 monsters, hiding spots, 1-4 player lobby, one cutscene.

## Risks
DOORS clones are many; the medieval plague setting and co-op roles must look different in the thumbnail (inferred). Keep scares kid-safe: no gore.
