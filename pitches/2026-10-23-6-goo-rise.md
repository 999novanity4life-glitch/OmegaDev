# Pitch 6: Goo Rise

**One sentence a kid gets:** Climb out of the factory before the candy goo gets you.

Data captured 2026-10-23 from Roblox's public games API (stats and votes) and rotrends/rowatcher (peaks). The full table is in `docs/research/PARTY_GAMES_2026-10-23.md`. ## Familiar Roblox loop (rising lava)
- **The Floor Is LAVA!**: 1,507 playing, 76.9% likes, 3.2B visits, 35.3K peak. Each cycle runs about 15 s prep, 40 s lava and 20 s intermission. https://www.roblox.com/games/815405518 ; https://earnaldo.com/blog/the-floor-is-lava-free-robux-guide
- **Escape The Lava**: 1,501 playing, 92.0% likes, 18K peak. https://www.roblox.com/games/140481779622210
- **100 Player Lava Escape**: 1,467 playing, 14K peak (Oct 2026). https://www.roblox.com/games/135535794227264

## Loop
- Goo rises over a 75 s round. Climb, grab gumdrops and survive.
- Crowns: 1 for escaping, +1 for a clean parkour route, +1 for finishing top 3.
- **Crowns unlock powers permanently**: Dash, Double Jump, Bubble Shield and Tongue Grapple.
- One power is equipped per round, on a cooldown.

## Where the depth comes from
- **Earned powers are our edge.** Every top lava game sells double jump, shield and grapple as Robux passes. Floor Is LAVA charges 39 R$ for Double Jump, 249 for Lava Shield and 499 for Grapple (https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/341654025/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=50).
- **No safe perch.** Factory machines break apart as goo rises, and candy blocks melt and fade.
- **Milestones.** Every 10 crowns, a short cutscene shows the factory's boss machine sputtering and opening a new floor.

## Feel and polish (SKILLS_VFX_RULEBOOK shapes)
- **Dash**: the Dash power, with afterimages and an FOV kick (kit numbers: 24 studs in 0.22 s).
- **Projectile**: Tongue Grapple, a tongue beam that pulls you to a hook.
- **Aura**: Bubble Shield (2 s bubble, 20 s cooldown). Goo glows and bubbles, and a red vignette appears near the surface.
- **Ground**: a goo splash ring when someone falls in. Melting tiles telegraph before they collapse.
- **Reveal**: a crown count-up pop on escape, and an unlock reveal for each new power.

## Monetization
- Sell: goo colours, trails, crown skins, escape effects and a VIP server.
- Never sell: powers, Spins, Lucky Blocks or Server Luck. The brainrot lava hybrids sell these (flagged in the catalog).

## v1 scope
- 3 factory maps.
- Goo driven by the server clock: clients compute `height(t)`, and the server checks deaths.
- 4 earnable powers.
- Crowns and a global leaderboard (OrderedDataStore, written once per round).
- Spectate and a mobile power button.

## Risks
- Classic lava games are stable but small, about 1.5K players each.
- The big 2026 money is in collect-and-rebirth hybrids (Escape Tsunami For Brainrots peaked at 4-5M). A collect layer such as a gumdrop shop may be needed later (inferred).
- "TOH but with rising lava" sits at 44.5% likes, so never make maps unfair or gear-gated.
