## Familiar Roblox loop
- **Natural Disaster Survival**: survive the round's disaster. Live since 2008, 4.19B visits, 90.8% likes, peak 78K (Mar 2025). https://rowatcher.com/games/65241/natural-disaster-survival

## Loop
Board the plane. Every 20 seconds something goes wrong (turbulence, engine fire, a door blows open, a goose in the cockpit). Fix it or hang on. If the plane lands, everyone still aboard wins.

## Where the depth comes from
- Ragdoll physics: passengers and luggage fly in turbulence.
- Takeoff and landing cutscenes; a crash cutscene when you fail, so failure is funny.
- New "routes" (snow airport, volcano island) change the disasters.

## Monetization
Pilot and crew outfits, plane liveries, a "first class" seat cosmetic, VIP server. Disasters stay random and free.

## v1 scope
1 plane, 8 disasters, 3-minute flight, landing win, crash cutscene, 2 routes.

## Risks
Don't copy Dear Passengers' name or art. Physics chaos must stay readable on mobile (inferred).
