## Loop
- A colour is called on a downbeat, and tiles of every other colour drop on the next beat.
- Calls speed up from every 6 s to every 3 s as the song builds.
- The last one dancing wins.
- Each song lasts 2 minutes.

## Where the depth comes from
- The music does the drama: build-ups, fake-out pauses (free, never sold) and a bonus "double colour" chorus.
- Every tile carries a symbol for colourblind players.

## Feel and polish (SKILLS_VFX_RULEBOOK shapes)
- **Ground**: tiles pulse on every beat; dropping tiles flash, then fall.
- **Aura**: the called colour glows around the arena edge.
- **Dash**: a dance-dash between tiles.
- **Projectile**: none in v1.
- **Reveal**: a podium with confetti on the song's final chord.

## Monetization
- Sell: dance emotes, tile-trail colours and cosmetic song packs.
- Never sell: ways to grief other players, or timer reveals.

## v1 scope
- 1 arena and 3 original tracks.
- Beats synced to the server clock (`workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`).
- Colourblind symbols on tiles.
- Bots.
