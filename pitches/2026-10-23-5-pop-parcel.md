**One sentence a kid gets:** Pass the wobbly present before it pops.

## Loop
- Touch another player to pass the parcel.
- Its 10 s timer doesn't reset on a pass, and the holder runs 10% faster.
- When it pops, the holder is out in a confetti burst.
- The last two duel on a shrinking rooftop.
- Wins and Ribbons (soft currency) buy cosmetics.
- A match takes about 2 minutes.

## Where the depth comes from
- **Movement skill.** Juke and "ankle-break" movement is the genre's most-watched YouTube content (1M views: https://youtu.be/WXfZdtVDB5w).
- **Tension.** The parcel wobbles more as time runs out, and holders get comic "uh-oh" animations.
- **Nobody sits out.** Eliminated players become ghosts who throw slow snowballs that nudge runners (inferred).

## Feel and polish (SKILLS_VFX_RULEBOOK shapes)
- **Projectile**: ghost snowballs (slow, harmless nudge).
- **Ground**: final-duel rooftop tiles heat up and crack (yellow → orange → red).
- **Dash**: afterimages show the holder's 10% speed boost.
- **Aura**: holder outline plus a pulsing ring that speeds up in the last 3 s, while the parcel beeps faster.
- **Reveal**: the pop. 70 ms hit-stop, confetti in the passer's chosen colour, 1.2° camera shake, and the kill effect credited to the passer.

## Monetization
- Sell: parcel wraps, pop effects, trails, emotes, and a VIP server with round controls.
- Never sell: gameplay items, tagger-chance passes or crates (chapter 14).

## v1 scope
- 1 lobby and 3 arenas.
- Touch-to-pass with server validation and a 0.5 s pass cooldown.
- Ghost snowballs and the final duel.
- Wins leaderboard and 6 pop effects.
- Bots fill in below 4 players.

## Risks
- One dominant incumbent (Timebomb Duels), and clones die fast (Monkey Bomb Tag 39K → 542).
- It must feel better in the first 10 seconds and launch with an ad burst (inferred).
- No brand names.
