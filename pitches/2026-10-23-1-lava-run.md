# Pitch 1: Slow-Mo Lava Run

**One sentence a kid gets:** Run up the tower before the lava gets you.

Data captured 2026-10-23. "Inferred" = my judgment, not measured.

## Why now (Steam signal)
- **KARLSON** (Dani): first-person parkour shooter with slow motion. 163K followers, #5 most-followed upcoming game on Steam, release TBA. https://profitable.app/steam/upcoming and https://steamdb.info/upcoming/mostfollowed/ (163,024). Premise: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1228610/discussions
- No date means the hype keeps building and nobody owns "Karlson but on Roblox" yet (inferred).

## Familiar Roblox loop
- **Escape The Lava**: dodge rising lava, train speed, collect speed pets. 206.6M visits, 92% likes, peak 16K (Sep 2025). https://rowatcher.com/games/8504461817/escape-the-lava
- **Tower of Hell**: race to the top of a random obby. Peak 75K (Aug 2026), 29.2B visits. https://rowatcher.com/games/703124385/tower-of-hell

## Loop
Lava rises. Climb. Reach the top, get a win and coins. Spend coins on speed, jump and trails. Repeat on a harder tower.

## Where the depth comes from (animation and cutscenes)
- Slide, wall-run and vault animations; screen goes slow-mo when you dive through glass or barely beat the lava.
- Ragdoll and a splash when the lava catches you.
- Every 10 wins: a short cutscene at the top where a boss knocks the tower over and you ride the collapse to the next world.

## Monetization
Trails and win effects, VIP server (choose tower), 2x wins pass, speed pets from eggs with every odd shown (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Skip-stage as a developer product.

## v1 scope
3 tower themes, rising lava, slide + wall-run + slow-mo, wins leaderboard, 1 egg of 8 pets.

## Risks
Don't use the name Karlson, milk, or Dani's character (IP). Lava games are common, so the slow-mo movement must be felt in the first 10 seconds (inferred).
